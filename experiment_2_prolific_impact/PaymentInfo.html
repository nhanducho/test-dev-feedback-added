Your Objective

The primary objective in this experiment is to choose the spending amount in each round that will maximize the total profit across all 100 rounds.

Your Payment Determination

At the end of the decision-making game, your final profit will be calculated and converted into Pound (£) at the conversion rate of 1111 ECU = £1.

This amount is your performance-based bonus from the decision-making game. It will then be added to your fixed participant fee of £3.

After completing the game, you will complete two short supplementary tasks. These tasks provide you with opportunities to earn more money. The earnings from these tasks will also be added to your final payment. After these tasks, you will answer a short demographic questionnaire before receiving your payment.

Please note that your final profit from the decision-making game may be negative if you experience many disruption losses and/or spend way too much on resilience. If your final profit from the decision-making game is negative after 100 rounds, your performance bonus from the main game will be set to £0.
