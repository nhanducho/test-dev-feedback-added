WELCOME TO THE SUPPLY CHAIN RISK MANAGEMENT EXPERIMENT

This game is an experiment about decision-making in supply chain risk management. The instructions are simple, and if you read them carefully and make good decisions, you could earn a considerable amount of money which will be paid to you in cash at the end of the experiment. To ensure that the instructions are clear and understandable, you are asked to answer a couple of multiple-choice questions after completing the reading. The real experiment will proceed once you complete all questions correctly. During the experiment, you are not allowed to make noise or talk to other participants. The first violation of this rule will result in a warning, and the second violation will result in immediate termination of participation; in this case, your earnings will be 0. If you have any questions during the experiment, please notify the instructor to receive the support needed.
