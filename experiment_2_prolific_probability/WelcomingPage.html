WELCOME TO THE SUPPLY CHAIN RISK MANAGEMENT EXPERIMENT

General Information

This game is an experiment on decision-making in supply chain risk management.

Please read the instructions carefully. The task is simple, but your decisions will directly affect your earnings. Since you may earn a substantial performance-based bonus in addition to a fixed participation fee, which will all be paid at the end of the experiment.

To ensure you understand the task, you will be asked to answer several multiple-choice comprehension questions after reading the instructions. You can start the experiment only after answering all questions correctly.
